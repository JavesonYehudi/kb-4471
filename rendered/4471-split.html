What each problem is

parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant's dates come out with the month and the day flipped. This is the bug the ticket names, and the fix is queued.

hint — through the booking-v3 form, the hint under the date field prints month first. The tenant's people type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The flip happens in the form, not in the parser: a different bug. A queued parser fix will not cover it.

one-day move — the booking lands one day off, not a month off. An invite that says Tuesday shows as Monday, and one meeting showed Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, and the company held it twice. None of the five bodies describe a month and day flip.
